# Caplet design system

Caplet is a calm, paper-like learning workspace. Preserve the current Bricolage/Hanken type pairing, warm surfaces, blue accent, rounded geometry, and restrained handwritten kickers. The dashboard should prioritize information density without becoming clinical: shallow cards, clear hierarchy, touch targets of at least 44px, visible focus states, and no raw colors outside the existing CSS variables.

For this dashboard redesign:
- Keep the Study/Money switch unchanged.
- Replace the two oversized streak and next-action heroes with a compact shared row.
- Each item must be independently dismissible and the choice must persist on the device.
- When any item is hidden, provide a quiet restore control that does not consume a full row.
- Preserve the exact practice-resume route and learning event tracking.
- Work in both Paper light and Paper dark; Pure white dark must inherit Paper dark.
